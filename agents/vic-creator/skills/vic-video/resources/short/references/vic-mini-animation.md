# VIC presenter identity and registration

Identity authority remains `../assets/vic-mini-v1/master-front.png`: hood/glasses, warm unmasked face, compact cartoon body and cream shoes. Preserve legacy artwork but do not auto-select tall/experimental rig prototypes. The current calibrated renderer/template retains the exact clean-pose PNGs and per-pose mouth metadata used in recent accepted-style films.

Normal staging is low-left with head/hands visible and captions clear. The prior 225px source-image width made the actual opaque body only99–142px wide on a1080px frame; do not judge visibility from the PNG canvas or file dimensions. Each job shot may set `presenter: {"x": 28, "y": 1578, "width": 315}`; choose larger host staging, smaller support staging, or `hidePresenter: true` from the content. Omitted values use the supporting315px/x28/y1578 fallback, not an instruction to show a large host everywhere. The same image-to-mouth transform follows the selected position/size. Preparation rejects invalid/off-canvas layouts; browser QA checks presenter/subtitle overlap. Choose by attention: a host-led explanation may use a larger reserved stage; an active demo may use a small support figure; dense evidence or a self-explanatory product result may remove it. Do not cycle sizes without a content/cut motivation. This fallback is a supporting presenter, not a universal host composition. Inspect the actual visible face/body at phone size across every selected pose, not merely an unclipped image box. A hero/host beat may need a separately composed larger presenter with relocated visuals/captions; do not blindly scale over evidence/subtitles or assume the default solves every layout. Hide the presenter when a narrative character or demo owns attention. Pose and gesture follow intent; maintain gaze/pointing toward the subject, consistent foot/neck/shoulder anchors, readable holds and clean entry/return. Don't cycle every pose or rock the whole sticker continually.

Source image, crop, scale and mouth calibration are one binding. Apply the exact image-to-canvas transform to the mouth. Different art/crop requires new measurement; don't copy old lip coordinates. Check neutral/gesture extremes, open speech and closed pause in encoded face crops. Reject chin/skin-patch animation, duplicate mouths/hands, detached cuffs and size jumps.

Drive mouth from narration-only envelope, closing through pauses/tail even if SFX continue. This is stylized audio-energy speech, not Burmese phoneme sync. Raster pose changes are not continuous finger/skeletal acting. Use [character motion](character-motion.md) only for separately calibrated narrative artwork, not as an excuse to reuse its landmark coordinates on VIC. Read [production mechanics](production-mechanics.md) for current source bindings.
