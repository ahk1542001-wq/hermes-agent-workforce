# Narrative character motion — local2D rig

Use `scripts/cartoon_motion.js` through the retained template when a still cartoon needs a blink, restrained head response, palm/finger movement or paper/folder action. It deforms the original texture locally; it is not full-body acting, new footage, a complete page turn or phoneme lip-sync. Choose a different authorized method only if the shot requires it. Do not satisfy acting with whole-image translation/zoom or a loop of pose names.

Configure each asset in native image pixels: `head:[pivotX,pivotY,radiusX,radiusY]`, `eyes:[[cx,cy,rx,ry],...]`; optional `hand:[elbowX,elbowY,centerX,centerY,rx,ry]`, `finger`, `page`, `file`, `robot`/`robotEyes`. Optional centers/radii use `[cx,cy,rx,ry]`. These are measured landmarks for THAT file/crop, never copied RAG coordinates. Inspect the source image and composite; keep screen direction and hands/props in contact.

Set per-asset `cues` with arrays of `{start,duration}` in the same time basis supplied to `drawCartoon(c,key,x,y,width,time)`: `blink`, `robotBlink`, `nod`, `hand`, `finger`, `page`, `file`, `robot`. No implicit repeated schedule: without cues it holds still. Choose brief, non-repeating reactions from the script, allow quiet holds and preserve continuous cues across adjacent shots. Independent characters may use different cues. The narration presenter uses its own measured speech envelope; don't make story characters falsely speak the narrator's words.

Typical code: `drawCartoon(c,'story_actor',40,600,1000,t);` with cues in global film time, or pass a consistent local cue time. Do not reset into the same blink on every cut. Optional `motionScale` scales displacement to the actual artwork; start small and inspect. Keep node movement bounded. The template uses shared-vertex textured WebGL triangles to avoid raster-patch seams. If its context is unavailable, fail explicitly; do not install/change global GPU settings or deliver a seamed fallback.

Before reuse inspect open/closing/closed/reopening eyes, rest/gesture extremes/settling, original alpha silhouette and hand-object contact. Check triangle orientation across the actual cue range and encoded samples at those times, not just t0. New landmarks are unverified until tested. Stills/geometry do not prove temporal appeal or continuous playback; view short normal-speed encoded clips where available and record actual review scope. Preserve original drawings and the previous master.

Calibrate each newly generated narrative illustration from its own native dimensions and visible landmarks. `story_actor` is an arbitrary job alias, not a packaged character. Prior RAG/test art or coordinates must not become defaults for a new topic. Preserve the original VIC presenter rig separately; flexible story characters and environments come from the current approved art direction.
