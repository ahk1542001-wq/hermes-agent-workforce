---
name: commercial-video
description: Plan, coordinate, edit and review a human-directed product film from approved assets.
---

1. Record the audience, format, duration, product reference and intended payoff.
   Present a beat map and get the owner's story approval before sourcing new shots.
2. Give each shot an ID, purpose, angle, action and expected duration. Record the
   correct product marks, wardrobe and proportions. Give any worker only the
   specific files, deliverable and checks needed for its task.
3. Select authorized source media first. When a new take is needed, record the exact
   references, destination, model settings and spend for owner approval. One
   operator owns the generation session. Reconcile an uncertain result before retrying.
4. Record source ranges in the edit plan. Cut together distinct shots. Design
   captions, accurate brand artwork and motion graphics in post. Keep music,
   source audio and effects separate so a visual revision can preserve the mix.
5. Inspect visible product text at delivery size, motion across every repaired
   interval, scene boundaries and continuity. Verify the exported file decodes,
   its duration and audio are correct, and its source ranges match the edit.
6. Deliver a review cut and, when requested, clean clips with source audio.
   Record what was checked and what still needs creative review. The owner
   decides whether to release the work.
