# Encoded QA and bounded resume

Review the exact candidate/hash against its current brief, approved script and sources. Separate encoding, facts/comprehension, layout, requested motion/anatomy, captions, audio and mouth evidence. Main Creator owns the final post-render review phase and checks worker artifacts itself; do not dispatch a QA subagent or QA bot. Keep review evidence tied to the exact encoded hash. A worker PASS never grants owner approval.

Probe dimensions/fps/codecs/audio/duration, decode the full file, reconcile full approved narration coverage, selected display-text mode/timing and input hashes. Verify loaded glyph/background bounds at phone and full resolution. Inspect every real cut/pause/tail and relevant speech/silence/gesture extrema. For localized cartoon motion inspect open/closed/reopened eyes and hand-object contact; for causal actions inspect encoded movement intervals. Source text, stills, pixel differences and decode do not prove continuous playback/listening, meaningful action or phoneme sync.

Assess pace and visual progression in the exact encoded output, including representative middle/late scenes. Compare planned action end times to shot length to locate possible entrance-only holds; these are review flags, not automatic semantic failures. Inspect/play the flagged intervals: foreground state change, focused detail/demo/result, meaningful transition and time to read. Narrator mouth/caption motion, pixel differences, one popup, camera wobble or a moving background do not establish that the explanation progresses. Record unmotivated static intervals with timecodes and local fixes; preserve an intentional reading/result hold when justified. Check that product claims are accompanied by authentic readable screens/output evidence or clearly disclosed conceptual visuals, with useful framing rather than arbitrary picture counts. Do not certify production polish from mechanical PASS or opening-only review. If real temporal/audio review is unavailable, record that limit instead of inventing a watch/listen report.

Inspect the settled result before intentional exit at phone size: persistent delivered items must retain expected opacity, readable contrast and organized receiver contact; entrance/mid-action frames are insufficient. Official screenshots need a readable subject crop, not a tiny full page.

Check graphic claims as well as narration: no invented cutoff dates, assurance badges or fictional sources passed as real. A visible sample answer must follow its source passage. Confirm suspected Burmese OCR defects against actual source and encoded glyphs before rewriting text. Reject incomplete/truncated media-model analysis; bounded retries end at NOT VERIFIED, not invented PASS. API parameters are model-specific, not a frozen universal thinking-budget recipe.

Record candidate hash, inspected times/sampling/capabilities, timecoded defects, verified checks and explicit unavailable coverage. Use [reviewer-handoff](reviewer-handoff.md) only if a structured report is useful. Preserve missing listening/motion as not_verified, not a pass; deliver with disclosed limits for owner review where appropriate. Only owner acceptance closes the creative gate.

Resume from compatible on-disk artifacts/manifest and verified hashes, actual process state and stop reason. Timeout/ACK/UI Finished is not artifact completion. One source writer/renderer; don't replay an old job or start a duplicate. Changed speech invalidates dependent timing; changed master invalidates its review. Maximum two unsuccessful repairs per concrete defect, then report missing capability. Don't impose the retired options/selection production-state schema on an existing job.

Use the [delivery evidence contract](visual-plan.md) for new production. Run check_job.py with --movie, then deliver_job.py with the main orchestrator's attributable post-render encoded per-shot review (role main-orchestrator-reviewer, review_phase post_render). Legacy independently authored reports remain supported. Missing assets/actions, source-only QA, missing reviewer evidence or unreviewed shots leave delivery blocked; do not set manifest status=completed to bypass it. These guards bind evidence and cannot authenticate invented observations: only record what the real reviewer inspected.


For new sound-bound builds, encoded QA also checks actual integrated loudness within1.5LU of the chosen plan and true peak within0.5dB of its ceiling; failed audio levels prevent mechanical PASS. This tolerance allows codec variation, not clipping permission. Check BGM ducking/masking, purposeful SFX and transition/event sync by listening. Sound-plan omission reasons must match the approved treatment. Main proof/final review includes sound; never infer pleasant sound or speech completeness from meters alone.

## Adversarial acceptance beyond compliance

Freeze the owner-accepted local candidate by exact path/hash in existing job metadata; publishing remains separate. Further general workflow improvements do not reopen that accepted film. Distinguish a defect-repair PASS from an overall production-quality claim. Before calling a new product explainer ready, challenge audience usefulness, promise/payoff, authentic product/output evidence, visual variety and causal progression, phone legibility/host presence, sound treatment and honest review coverage. A renderer with zero layout errors can still produce a boring slide deck. A mechanical threshold or one popup cannot certify engagement.

For the owner audience, test the practical agent-assisted workflow against the script: user task/input, agent responsibility, software/runtime action, preview/human check and useful output. Verify every asserted integration; label proposals and conceptual visuals. Do not let repo metadata or license exposition consume the main payoff. Check presenter visibility from the actual visible body/face and phone-size frame rather than transparent canvas dimensions; verify every pose and mouth/gesture scaling after registration changes. Missing continuous listening/playback remains explicitly unverified. Document prioritized defects and a smallest useful before/after proof instead of piling up more generic instructions or effects.

For the next-topic transfer test, main freezes a new audience/problem/payoff, topic-specific assets and scene/action/sound packet. Reuse mechanics, not the previous cast, palette, cue timings or shot sequence. Exercise the chosen style in a representative real-audio proof, including a mechanism/result beat rather than only the hook. Judge fresh-topic clarity, causal action, text/host clearance, sound sync and within-film variation against that packet. A read-only readiness reply or skill validator is not a successful new-video test.
