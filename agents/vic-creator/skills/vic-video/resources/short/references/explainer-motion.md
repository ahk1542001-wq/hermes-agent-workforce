# Purposeful object motion

Choose what viewers should notice and what changes: selection, transfer, search/highlight, connection, comparison or consequence. Plan anticipation/entrance → action/result → readable hold → exit. Narration controls timing; text/camera should hold while reading. Use deterministic seekable cues rather than wall-clock randomness.

Preserve spatial relationships through continuing ideas. A cut, reveal, stable-node carry, restrained slide or close-up is a choice, not a mandatory transition per beat. Keep gaze/pointing toward the actual subject, legible captions and whole head/hands. Sprite wobble, caption changes and repetitive icon-arrow chains alone are not causal explanation.

Use the current brief's palette/background. Keep accents subordinate; no retired landmark/theme or forced greeting from an old script. Use [character motion](character-motion.md) for narrative figures and [presenter registration](vic-mini-animation.md) for VIC. A pose swap remains a pose swap; don't claim independent limb or phoneme motion. Check extrema and cut boundaries in the actual encode.
